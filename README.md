# AI-101: Work with AI like you work with a teacher

**Open the site:** <https://ej-kim-dev.github.io/AI_101/>

AI-101 is a short guide, in English and Korean (한국어), for people who are new to AI tools like Claude. It's written for high-school teachers, and it explains AI by comparing it to working with a teacher.

The whole page follows one example: a teacher making a slide that introduces IB Computer Science SL, using the IB Computer Science guide.

## What it covers

- **Main ideas:** chats, files, projects, folders and connectors (such as Google Slides, Canva and Google Drive)
- **Chat vs. project:** when to use a normal chat and when a project saves you time
- **RICE:** writing a clear prompt with a **R**ole, an **I**nstruction, **C**ontext and an **E**xample
- **Example:** an unclear request next to a clear one, and what each gives you back
- **Try it yourself:** copy the example prompt and try it in Claude

## How to use it

- **Scroll** down the page, or click a section in the menu at the top to jump to it.
- **Switch language** with the English / 한국어 switch at the top right. To share the Korean version, use <https://ej-kim-dev.github.io/AI_101/?lang=ko>.
- **Copy prompt** copies the example prompt, so you can paste it into Claude and see what you get.
- It works on computers, tablets and phones, in light and dark mode.
